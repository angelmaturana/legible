Example

A page you can copy

This page shows the layout that every inner page of the site can follow: an introduction, two sections of text, a grid of cards and a closing call to action.

First section

Use the first section to say what the page is about. Keep paragraphs short, start with the most important sentence and avoid claims that you cannot prove. A visitor who reads only this section should already know whether the page is useful to them.

When you copy this page, change the folder name, the title and the description in the front matter, and the text of every section. The address of the page, its date and its structured data are filled in automatically from the folder and from the line that starts with "updated".

Second section

Use the second section for the detail: how something works, what is included, what is not, or what the visitor needs to prepare. Lists are useful when the items are parallel:

  • One idea per item, written as a short sentence.
  • No figures, prices or schedules unless you can confirm them.
  • Links to other pages of the site when they help the reader go further.

A grid of cards

Cards are a compact way to show three or four parallel items, such as topics, steps or questions that come up often.

First card

A short description of the first item. Two sentences are enough.

Second card

A short description of the second item. Keep every card about the same length.

Third card

A short description of the third item. Remove a card if you have nothing real to put in it.

Ready to continue?

Close the page with one clear next step. On most pages it is the same: write to us.
