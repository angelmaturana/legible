Our perspective

Opinion pieces on TechFlow Blog explore the deeper implications of technology choices, architectural decisions, and industry trends. These articles are written from production experience, not speculation. We write about trade-offs we've encountered, patterns we've observed, and decisions we've made — and why. We're not afraid to challenge conventional wisdom when our experience suggests a different path.

Every opinion piece is grounded in real-world practice. We admit uncertainty where it exists and acknowledge that context matters. What works for a small team may not scale; what works at scale may be overkill for a startup. We publish these pieces when we have something substantive to say, not on a schedule. We welcome disagreement — the best technical discussions come from conflicting experience.

We clearly label opinion pieces as distinct from our reviews, tutorials, and analysis so readers always know what they're getting. Each piece includes the author's relevant background so you can assess their perspective.
