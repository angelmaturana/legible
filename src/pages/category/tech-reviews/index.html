Our review methodology

Every review on TechFlow Blog is based on hands-on testing in real-world scenarios. We don't just read specifications or rewrite press releases — we install, configure, and use the products we review. Our reviews include detailed benchmarks, performance measurements, and practical observations that only come from actual usage. We test hardware under sustained workloads, evaluate software in production-like environments, and compare services against alternatives.

We publish our full methodology for each review category. When firmware or software updates significantly change a product's behavior, we revisit and update our reviews. We accept no sponsored content, and affiliate relationships never influence our conclusions. Each review includes a clear recommendation: buy, wait, or skip, based on the evidence we gathered.

Our review process typically takes one to two weeks of daily use before we publish. This ensures we catch issues that only appear over time — thermal throttling, battery degradation, driver stability, and long-term usability concerns.
