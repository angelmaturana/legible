Contact

Get in touch with {{site_name}}

Here is how to reach us. This page explains what to include in your first message so that the answer can be as useful as possible.

How to reach us

Email is the best way to send details, links or attachments, and it keeps a written record of the conversation for both sides. A chat message is handy for a quick question, and a phone call works best when the matter is urgent. Use whichever channel is listed below that suits you; they all reach the same people.

{{contact_methods}}

What to include in your first message

With a few details we can give you a useful answer from the start:

  • Your name and, if it applies, the name of your organisation.
  • What you need or what you would like to achieve.
  • Any links or documents that help us understand the situation.
  • The best way and the best time to reach you.

What happens next

We read every message and answer it ourselves. If your request is urgent, say so in the first line so that we can see it straight away. If we need more information before we can help, we will ask for it in our reply, and if we are not the right people for what you need, we will tell you that as well.

What we do with your message

We use your details only to answer you. The privacy policy explains what data we keep, for how long and how to ask us to delete it.
