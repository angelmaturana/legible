Frequently asked questions

How long do you take to reply?

We typically reply within a few business days. If your message is urgent, use "URGENT" in the subject line.

Do you accept guest posts?

We rarely accept guest contributions. If you have a specific piece in mind, pitch us the outline and we'll consider it.

Can I advertise or sponsor content?

No. We don't publish sponsored content, native advertising, or paid reviews. Our editorial independence is not for sale.

I found an error in an article. How do I report it?

Email us with the article URL, the specific error, and the correct information. We publish corrections promptly.
