Cookie Policy

This website does not set cookies and does not use trackers, visitor statistics or advertising tools.

This text is a template that describes the sample site as it is built. If you add anything that stores or reads data in the visitor's browser, update it before you publish and have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website saves in your browser to remember information about your visit. Similar technologies, such as local storage, work in the same way and are covered by this policy too.

2. What this website uses

This website does not set any cookies, first-party or third-party, and does not use local storage or similar technologies.

  • No visitor statistics, advertising or social media tracking tools are installed.
  • The pages run no scripts in your browser. The mobile menu works with plain HTML and CSS.
  • Fonts and images are served from the same domain as the pages, not from an external service.

Because nothing is stored on your device, there is no cookie banner and nothing to accept or reject.

3. Links to other services

The email and WhatsApp buttons are plain links (mailto: and wa.me/), not embedded widgets, so they do not load anything from those services while you read a page.

If you follow a link to another website or open WhatsApp, that service applies its own cookie policy, which we do not control.

4. Managing cookies in your browser

You can allow, block or delete cookies for any website in your browser settings. Look for the cookies option in the privacy or security section of the settings of your browser, or search its help pages for "delete cookies". This applies to every site you visit, not only this one.

5. If cookies are added later

Adding a contact form with a session, a visitor statistics tool, an embedded map or video, a chat widget or any advertising pixel changes the answer above. Before you publish such a change:

  • List every cookie or storage key in this page: name, who sets it, purpose, duration and whether it is strictly necessary.
  • Check whether the law that applies to you requires prior consent. If it does, add a consent mechanism that lets visitors accept or reject non-essential cookies, and load those tools only after consent.
  • Update the privacy policy so it names the new tool and the data it collects.
  • Replace the statements in sections 2 and 3 and in the lead of this page, and change the updated date in the page header.

6. Changes to this policy

We will update this page whenever the way the website stores data in your browser changes.
