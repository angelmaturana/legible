What we do

Describe your main activity in plain words. Write what a person receives, not how you feel about it, and give only facts you can stand behind.

A good home page answers four questions: what is this, who is it for, why should I trust it and what do I do next. Each of the sections below answers one of them. Delete the ones you do not need and keep the rest short. The example page shows a longer layout that you can copy for every other page of the site.

Who it is for

Name the people you work with and the situations in which they come to you. A sentence such as "we help people who need X when Y happens" is clearer than a list of adjectives. If there are people you do not work with, say so: it saves time for everyone.
